# The minimum norm of a single implementer under stabilization

**Work in progress, November 2026.** This is a written-checked research note, not a submission or peer-reviewed paper. No Lean verification or priority claim is made.

Let \(X\ne\varnothing\) be locally compact Hausdorff and \(\Gamma\) discrete. For \(A=C_0(X)\otimes\mathcal K(\ell^2)\) with any \(\Gamma\)-action, the specified central-sequence derivation on the full or reduced crossed product has the same minimum implementer norm as for the induced action on \(C_0(X)\). No invariant finite-rank constant corner is needed. When the derivation is inner, that minimum is \(2(1-1/n)\) for \(|\Gamma|=n<\infty\) and \(2\) for an infinite group. These are bounds for the specified test, not for all derivations into all admissible modules.

Write \(M=A^{**}\), \(Z=Z(M)\), \(N=\ell^\infty(\Gamma,Z)\), \(U=N/\iota Z\) for the quotient by constant fields, and \(S=\mathbb B(\ell^2\Gamma)\bar\otimes M\). Write \(q:N\to U\) for the quotient and \(d(F)_{t,t}=F(t^{-1})\). In the center-linear covariant predual quotient of \(U\widehat\otimes_\pi S_*\), the test is \(\Delta(b)(qF)=[\rho(b),d(F)]\), where \(\rho\) is the regular representation. The all-positive right \((G_2)\) bound on this predual has constant one: the normal functional estimate uses Cauchy–Schwarz and \(\sum_i a_i^2\le\|\sum_i a_i\|\sum_i a_i\) for positive test elements. It passes to the projective tensor product and to the quotient by using a common lift for each finite family.

The center \(Z\) identifies equivariantly with \(C_0(X)^{**}\): compress to a fixed rank-one multiplier corner and recover the center by constant amplification over the multiplier matrix units. This identifies the actions on the center even if individual corner projections move. The expectation characterization of action amenability, and nuclearity of the crossed product in this nuclear-base case, are standard inputs; see [Ozawa–Suzuki (2021), Theorem 3.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00029-021-00699-2) and [Anantharaman–Delaroche (1987)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01461725).

For an implementer \(T\), \(Y_F=d(F)-T(qF)\) commutes with the regular image. A positive contractive approximate identity \(e_\lambda\) of \(A\) yields \(e_\lambda u_g\) in the crossed product, with regular images converging strongly* to the group unitary. Thus \(Y_F\) commutes with those unitaries and all its diagonal entries are equal. Commutation with the coefficient algebra implies that this common entry \(L(F)\) belongs to \(Z\). It fixes constant fields and is center-linear and equivariant. The group-index diagonal of \(T\) is then the implementer \(qF\mapsto d(F)-1\otimes L(F)\), with norm no larger. Its norm depends only on \(U,Z,L\), so the commutative and stabilized settings have the same minimum. Full and reduced have the same test and implementers because the regular map factors through the reduced quotient.

For the lower bound, let \(b_t\) be the field \(1_Z\) at \(t\) and zero elsewhere. For \(|\Gamma|\ge2\), \(\|qb_t\|=1/2\). For a state \(\phi\) on \(Z\), looking at entry \(t^{-1}\) gives \(\|T\|\ge2|1-\phi L(b_t)|\). For \(n\) elements some \(\operatorname{Re}\phi L(b_t)\le1/n\), since \(L\) fixes constants. The uniform expectation attains \(2(1-1/n)\) by the diameter bound for the average of \(n\) points. The trivial group gives zero. For an infinite group, the absolute values \(|\phi L(b_t)|\) sum to at most \(\|\phi L\|\) over every finite subset, by choosing scalar phases. Hence they get arbitrarily small and \(\|T\|\ge2\). An equivariant contractive expectation attains two by subtracting constant fields before taking the quotient norm.

## Continuous-trace coefficients

The same comparison holds for a nonzero continuous-trace \(C^\ast\)-algebra \(A\) with spectrum \(X\) and an arbitrary discrete group action, including nontrivial Dixmier–Douady class. For the same specified right-\(G_2\) derivation on the full or reduced crossed product, innerness is equivalent to topological amenability of \(\Gamma\curvearrowright X\); in that case the minimum implementer norm is \(2(1-1/n)\) for \(|\Gamma|=n<\infty\) and \(2\) for infinite \(\Gamma\).

The point is that the nondegenerate central multiplier action of \(C_0(X)\) extends to an equivariant normal isomorphism \(C_0(X)^{\ast\ast}\to Z(A^{\ast\ast})\). Locally a continuous-trace algebra is Morita equivalent over the spectrum to \(C_0(V)\). In the bidual of the local linking algebra both diagonal projections have full central support, which identifies their centers via the local scalar action. As \(V\) ranges over the open cover the support projections of \(C_0(V)\) have supremum \(1\). Their images are the central supports of the corresponding ideals of \(A\), so the local identifications give an injective and surjective normal map globally. Equivariance follows on the scalar multipliers and then by normal extension. No algebra trivialization or invariant finite-rank corner is assumed.

The existing center-valued conditional expectation criterion for the derivation therefore reduces to the commutative system (see [Anantharaman-Delaroche (1987)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01461725) and [Ozawa–Suzuki (2021), Theorem 3.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00029-021-00699-2)). The norm calculation is unchanged: the implementation gives a bounded retraction on the **center** with constant functions fixed; the single-coordinate lower bound and the upper bound from the contractive expectation use no trivialization data. This is a written-checked corollary of the general discrete-action construction and the preceding norm argument, not a Lean result or priority claim.
